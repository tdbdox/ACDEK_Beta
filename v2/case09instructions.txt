Benchmark Case 09 — Multi-Objective Prioritization and Constraint Resolution

Purpose:
This benchmark evaluates ACDEK's ability to manage multiple overlapping objectives within a single session. It is designed to test the agent's prioritization logic, constraint balancing, and memory-informed tradeoff decisions in scenarios where conflicting goals are present. This case simulates real-world operational tension—such as balancing urgency, strategic value, and risk.

Context:
The user presents multiple requests with varying levels of urgency and strategic importance. ACDEK must prioritize, resolve constraints, and deliver a coherent output that respects the context, user traits, environmental volatility, and embedded memory. This benchmark validates how well the system:
- Recognizes competing objectives
- Makes context-aware tradeoffs
- Balances short-term urgency vs long-term strategy
- Leverages prior memory to make better decisions

Validation Metrics:
1. Goal disambiguation and objective ranking
2. Conflict detection between objectives
3. Successful constraint resolution (e.g. time, resources)
4. Modulation of decision logic based on Cu and Ef
5. Coherent, actionable prioritization in the output
6. Memory-informed decision influence
7. Clarity of gate decision reasoning
8. Slot payload richness with clear prioritization metadata
9. Consistency of semantic class across decision layers

Passing Criteria:
A session passes if the final output ranks objectives based on urgency, impact, and user context; resolves key conflicts; and produces a deterministic execution trace with traceable rationale.
